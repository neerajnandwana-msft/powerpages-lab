# Lab 01 – Your First Power Pages Site

In this lab you will create a basic Power Pages site from scratch.

## Objectives

- Provision a new Power Pages site
- Explore the design studio
- Publish your first page

## Steps

### 1. Open Power Pages

Navigate to [make.powerpages.microsoft.com](https://make.powerpages.microsoft.com) and sign in with your work account.

### 2. Create a new site

Click **+ New site**, choose a template, and follow the wizard.

### 3. Explore the studio

Familiarise yourself with the **Pages**, **Styling**, and **Data** panels.

### 4. Publish

Click **Preview** to review your site, then **Publish** to make it live.

## Summary

You've created and published your first Power Pages site. Continue to the next lab to learn about customising the layout.
